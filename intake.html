Before your first visit

A few questions about your life, not just your symptoms.

This takes about fifteen minutes. Most questions are multiple choice. Many describe an everyday situation, and you pick the reaction that sounds most like you. There are no right answers.

  • You can skip any question.
  • You never have to give details. There is a space to add more if you want to.
  • Your answers are not a diagnosis. They help your clinician know where to start.

This is a demonstration. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere.

Which sounds most like you?

You don't have to wait for your appointment. If you are in the U.S., you can call or text 988 any time to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Your clinician will see this answer first.

All done

Thank you. Here's what happens next.

  • Your clinician reads your answers before your first session, so you don't have to start from the beginning.
  • They may ask a few follow-up questions. You can say no to any of them.
  • Nothing here is a diagnosis. Only a trained clinician can make one, and only after talking with you.
  • You can change any answer at your visit.
If you are in the U.S. and need someone now, call or text 988. You don't have to wait for your appointment.
